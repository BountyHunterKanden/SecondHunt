How to

Pull: left-drag a hand, forearm, foot or shin and the limb bends after it, like a doll's arm (shoulder / hip, elbow / knee).

Spin: right-drag a piece and it turns about its joint as if in your hand, on every axis; drag near the edge of the screen circle round it to twist. With Children follow on, everything hanging off it turns along (joints too, so nothing comes apart when it moves); off, the piece turns alone.

Mirror copies every change to the other side. Rest is a clean A-pose; Vanilla standing shows the same corrections in the game's own stance.

Play runs the game's real clips on your working copy, exactly as the Odin will. On empty space: left-drag orbits, right-drag pans, wheel zooms.
