How to use

Left is the game model, split into the chunks each bone moves rigidly. Right is the trophy, coloured by the bone each part follows. Left/right in bone names are the character's own sides.

Pick a bone (click a chunk on the game model or a row here), then click or drag on the trophy to hand it parts. Alt+click the trophy picks up the bone under the cursor. Drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom.

Seams: rigid moves the two chunks separately, like the game model; blend bends a continuous surface across a narrow band (width in % of body height).

Keep shape: paint it over parts that must never stretch (armour plates, blades, the head). Next to one, the soft side takes the whole bend. Shown pale.

Save & refit writes the grouping beside the trophy, refits the rig and updates the cache the app loads. Then preview the game's clips.
