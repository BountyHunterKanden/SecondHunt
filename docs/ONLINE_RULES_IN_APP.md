ONLINE PLAY: AGES 13 AND UP
---------------------------
Online play is for players 13 and older. Online, you play and talk with people you don't know: quick messages, typed
chat and voice. The campaign, bots and local multiplayer never go online.


PRIVACY
-------
Second Hunt is a free fan project. Your game files stay on your device and are never uploaded. Online play keeps only
what it needs, on our server in the US (hosted by Akamai / Linode):

  - your online profile key (a random key your device made; no email, phone or real name is ever asked for)
  - your hunter name and tag, friend code and Hunter License numbers
  - your friends, rivals, requests, blocks and recent players
  - how many online matches you've played, and any chat limit or ban on your profile
  - if your profile is banned: a scrambled ID of the device(s) it used, so the ban holds after a reinstall
    (until the ban ends)
  - reports: for a chat report, the last few typed lines of that game (kept 30 days once handled, 90 days if not)
  - a nightly backup of the above (kept 14 days)

Never kept: voice (never recorded), typed chat (only inside a report), IP addresses (only in memory while you're
connected), and nothing else from your device (your game sends a scrambled device ID when it logs in; it's only
kept if your profile is banned): no contacts, location, ads, analytics or trackers. Some matches pass
through Cloudflare's relay when two players can't connect directly; it sees your IP address, not your profile.

Your choices: mute, block or report anyone from their name or a chat line. Voice is off for strangers until you turn it
on. Hide yourself from name search, or delete your online profile, in ONLINE PLAY > SETTINGS.

Questions, or to have your data deleted: secondhuntgame@gmail.com


CODE OF CONDUCT
---------------
Play hard, talk friendly.

Don't:
  - harass, threaten or insult anyone, or go after someone for who they are
  - post sexual content, or anything sexual involving or aimed at someone under 18
  - ask for or share personal details (real names, addresses, schools, phone numbers, social media, photos)
  - try to meet up with someone, or move them to another app
  - spam, cheat, pretend to be someone else, pick an offensive name, or dodge a ban

Mute, block or report anyone who does. Breaking these rules can get your chat muted or your online profile banned.
Serious cases (threats, anything sexual involving children) are reported to the authorities.

Think a decision was wrong? Write to secondhuntgame@gmail.com with your hunter name and tag.
