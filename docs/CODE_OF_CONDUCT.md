# Second Hunt online play: code of conduct

Play hard, talk friendly. Online play means sharing arenas, lobbies and chat with people you don't know.

## Don't

- **Harass anyone:** no insults, threats, slurs, or going after someone for who they are (race, religion, gender,
  sexuality, disability, nationality or anything else).
- **Post sexual content**, or anything sexual involving or aimed at someone under 18. That one goes straight to a
  permanent ban and, where the law asks, to the authorities.
- **Ask for or share personal details:** real names, addresses, schools, phone numbers, social media, photos.
- **Try to meet up** with or move someone you met here to another app.
- **Spam:** flooding chat, advertising, links.
- **Cheat:** modified game data or tools that give you an edge, exploiting bugs on purpose, or messing with other
  players' connections.
- **Pretend to be someone else**, including the game's moderator.
- **Pick an offensive name.** The filter catches a lot, not everything.
- **Dodge a ban** with a new profile.

## Do

- **Mute** anyone you don't want to hear, **block** anyone you don't want to see, **report** anything above. A report
  from a chat line sends the moderator what was typed.
- Remember voice is off for strangers until you turn it on.

## What happens

The moderator (BountyHunterKanden) reads reports and decides. Depending on how serious it is: a warning, a chat
mute, a ban for some days, or a permanent ban of the online profile. A name that breaks the rules is replaced. Serious
cases (threats, anything sexual involving children) are reported to the authorities.

Think a decision is wrong? Write to secondhuntgame@gmail.com with your hunter name and tag.
